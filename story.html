Our Story

Where it all began
❦
The Question

Who would have thought that a simple question, “Can we walk together to the gate?” would become the beginning of a beautiful love story?

❦
The Walk

Our paths crossed in the university, where life brought two strangers together in the most ordinary yet extraordinary way.

What started as a simple walk to the gate soon became the beginning of conversations, laughter, friendship, and a bond that neither of us knew would one day mean so much.

❦
Growing Closer

With every conversation, we grew closer. What began as friendship slowly blossomed into something deeper, something beautiful, and something worth holding on to.

Without even realising it, we were building a connection that would grow beyond the walls of the university and become a love we would cherish for a lifetime.

❦
Looking Back

Looking back, it is amazing how one simple question became the opening chapter of our forever. A walk that could have ended at the gate became a journey that led us to each other, teaching us that sometimes, the most beautiful things in life begin with the smallest moments.

And now, here we are, ready to begin a new chapter, not just as two people who met by chance, but as two hearts who found friendship, discovered love, and chose a lifetime together.

Our Forever

Our journey began with a simple walk, but our destination has always been something beautiful: a lifetime of love, friendship, laughter, faith, and choosing each other, over and over again.

This is our story. This is our love. And this is the beginning of our forever. ❤️
